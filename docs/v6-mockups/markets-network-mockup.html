What the brush replaces

Four fixed range buttons, each one refetching. Here a superset is fetched once and windowed on the client — fewer requests against a Demo-tier key, and instant interaction.

Range buttons survive as presets that move the brush rather than swap datasets.

Why canvas

SVG caps out around a few hundred candles. This is — candles held at interactive framerates, and granularity steps with zoom — daily at 1Y, 4h at 90D, hourly at 30D.

The cost is DOM accessibility, which is exactly why D0847 is mandatory rather than optional.

The differentiator

Price charts are commodity. Price charts annotated with Monero's own history — the IRS bounty, the Binance delisting, tail emission, the FCMP++ testnet — are not.

It also ties Markets to Education structurally rather than by a link in a footer.

Health at a glance

Currently a visitor must already know what 2.81 GH/s means. With bands they need to know one thing: is the series inside its band. That is the biggest conceptual upgrade available to this page.

Every threshold documents its source inline — an undocumented band is just a coloured rectangle asserting authority it hasn't earned.

Streaming vs polling

The chart scrolls left as points arrive rather than redrawing on each poll — a block becomes an event on a timeline, not a number that changed.

Architect for the relay's WebSocket (P3) but ship on polling. Ideally the difference is a data-source swap, nothing more.

The unused advantage

Live data sitting next to the simulator that explains it. Cadence → Metronome. Difficulty/hashrate → Thermostat. Mempool fee bands → Orbital and Abyss.

No other Monero site can do this, and this one currently doesn't.
