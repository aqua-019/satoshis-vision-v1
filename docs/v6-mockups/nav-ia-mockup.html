What changed

Eleven top-level items wrapped on mobile and pushed users past scanning into reading. Six fit on one line at 720px and still cover everything — because Cmd+K makes depth free.

  • Live absorbs Mempool, Network, Markets — the three surfaces that update.
  • Learn absorbs Education and Simulate, which overlapped already.
  • Operate merges /node and /pro, near-duplicates where Pro linked out to the node guide.
  • About takes /sources and /peers — meta, not Monero.

The two decisions P5 forces

MarketsThesisTab duplicates /markets; OutlookTab duplicates /future. Merge or cross-link — but the same content must not live in two places.

Shown here as merged: the Monero dropdown lists seven chapters, not ten, and the two market/outlook tabs redirect into Live and Future.

A third one this mockup surfaced: type tail into the palette and Tail emission comes back twice — once as a Future protocol card, once as a Learn simulator. Same for FCMP++, Seraphis, Jamtis and CARROT. Decide whether each simulator is reachable only through its protocol card, or lives independently in Learn. Either is fine; two entries with the same name and different URLs is not.

Behaviour worth checking

  • D1542 hover intent — 150ms before a dropdown opens, 220ms before it closes. Cross a nav item on the way somewhere else and nothing fires.
  • D0235 morphing pill — the active marker slides between items rather than jumping.
  • Keyboard only — Tab to a nav item, ↓ opens it, Esc closes. Palette is ↑↓ ⏎ Esc, never needs a mouse.
  • D1207 container queries — the frame drives the layout, not the viewport, so this behaves the same inside PageShell as it does full-bleed.
